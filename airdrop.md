# VIRIO Early Community Distribution

The 100,000,000 VIRIO early-community allocation is a long-duration network bootstrap, not sale access, a holder reward or a trading incentive.

- **Genesis claim:** up to 10,000,000 VIRIO, after published eligibility and sybil review.
- **Progressive stream:** the remaining 90,000,000 VIRIO is earned from month 3 through month 24.
- **Eligible work:** successful testnet execution, merchant and SDK integrations, meaningful development, bug and security reports, ecosystem infrastructure and sustained protocol participation.
- **Anti-farming:** minimum contribution thresholds, quality review, funding/behaviour graph analysis, identity and referral caps, cohort analysis, rate limits, public methodology, reason codes and appeals.
- **Custody:** a fresh Base AirdropDistributor/vesting deployment is required before any claim. It must enforce the published caps, timing and allocation ceiling.

Rules are published before each distribution period. The program does not reward cheaply manufactured activity merely because it creates transactions.
