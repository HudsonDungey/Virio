# Phase 2 — Public Genesis Preparation

Virio does not depend on a private financing round or token-sale proceeds. This phase prepares a transparent public VIRIO genesis on Base.

- [ ] Establish Base multisig signers, 48-hour timelock and public treasury accounting.
- [ ] Deploy allocation custody and independent vesting contracts; founder allocation must be 0 liquid at TGE.
- [ ] Configure and publish the early-community eligibility, streaming and anti-sybil process.
- [ ] Determine and disclose GENESIS_LP_TOKEN_AMOUNT and GENESIS_LP_QUOTE_AMOUNT; do not exceed the 50M Protocol Launch Liquidity allocation.
- [ ] Keep bridges, fee distribution and buyback paths disabled at genesis.
- [ ] Publish deployment addresses, source verification, supply verification, allocation and audit/protocol status.
- [ ] Ensure the payment protocol is described as testnet/beta until professional security review is complete.

Founder or other private capital may fund professional review and development independently. That is not a public token-sale use of proceeds, a token-holder entitlement, or a protocol promise.
